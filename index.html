About the project

Built for secure, transparent, and collaborative digital twin systems.

DeDTs is a next-generation research platform that bridges Digital Twin innovation with decentralized ledger technology. It enables provenance-aware IP protection, secure data sharing, and trustable analytics for complex engineering and research domains.

Security by design

Immutable provenance and private collaboration.

Leverage blockchain immutability, zero-knowledge proofs, and federated learning to protect sensitive models and data while preserving transparency.

Modular architecture

Flexible components for digital twin creation.

Combine on-chain governance, decentralized storage, and IP-NFT frameworks to build composable digital twin services and collaborative research pipelines.

Capabilities

DeDTs Core Capabilities

Decentralized Data Management

Securely store Digital Twin assets and simulation data with IPFS, Filecoin, and distributed ledger indexing.

IP-NFT Provenance

Issue intellectual property tokens to record ownership, licensing, and research provenance in a tamper-proof way.

DAO Governance

Enable collaborative decision-making and resource allocation across stakeholders with decentralized governance.

Federated Learning

Improve predictive models using privacy-aware distributed training without centralizing raw data.

Decentralized Funding

Support research and product growth through NFT-based fundraising and DAO-backed incentives.

Confidentiality Enhancements

Adopt zero-knowledge proof and secret sharing methods to safeguard sensitive digital twin information.

Research Objectives

Strategic goals for next-generation digital twin ecosystems.

Our research plan focuses on solving foundational problems in digital twin adoption by combining trust, privacy, and sustainable governance into one platform.

O1

Assess Digital Twin challenges and Web3 applicability

Analyze where blockchain, decentralized storage, and tokenized IP yield the greatest benefit for digital twin workflows.

O2

Build a tamper-proof collaboration platform

Develop secure shared infrastructure for federated learning, data exchange, and cooperative model development.

O3

Create IP-NFT frameworks for provenance

Anchor intellectual property assets and fundraising mechanisms to verifiable digital tokens.

O4

Deliver transparent predictive analytics

Implement analytics pipelines with on-chain auditing, model validation, and trusted reporting.
